Ollie

A private AI secretary on WhatsApp.

Ollie is a personal assistant built and used by one person. It runs on its owner's own server and only replies to its owner's WhatsApp number. It is not offered to the public.

What Ollie does:

  • Answers text and voice messages on WhatsApp
  • Sets reminders
  • Keeps personal notes
  • Reads and manages its owner's Google Calendar: lists, adds, changes and removes events when asked
